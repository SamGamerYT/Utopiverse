**Patch/Changes 8.9.11.09** *(Network Changes)*

> :shiny: [Survival] Fixed an issue where **Temp Fly Duration** was incorrectly capped at **7 days**.
> :shiny: [Survival] Increased the **Random Teleportation** radius from **9000x9000** to **10000x10000** in the **Primary World**.
> :shiny: [Survival] Removed the **"/rt"**, **"/randomtel"**, and **"/wildtp"** commands.
> :shiny: [Survival] Reduced **Amber Sword** requirement from **Fighting 8** to **Fighting 4**.
> :shiny: [Survival] Increased **Amber Sword** durability from **500** to **512**.
> :shiny: [Survival] Increased the durability gained when upgrading **Steel Gear Set**, **Lost Miner Gear Set**, **Upgraded Miner Gear Set**, and **Refined Miner Gear Set** with **Metamorphic Rock** from **64** to **256**.
> :shiny: [Survival] Removed the **Confusion Key** from **Essence Trader**.
