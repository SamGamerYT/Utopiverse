> **DUNGEON MINE CHANGES**
> :shiny: [Survival] Introducing the **Copper Zone**, which is limited to players below **Power Level 40**.
> :shiny: [Survival] **Copper Zone** enemies have **15–20 Health** and deal **2–4 Damage**.
> :shiny: [Survival] All Copper enemies use unique Earth elemental abilities and drop no items when defeated.
> :shiny: [Survival] Added the Copperbound Zombie, featuring an Earth Strike and rewarding **12 Fighting XP**.
> :shiny: [Survival] Added the Oxidized Husk, featuring an Earth Slam and rewarding **20 Fighting XP**.
> :shiny: [Survival] Added the Verdigris Skeleton, featuring an Earth Arrow and rewarding **14 Archery XP**.
> :shiny: [Survival] Added the Copperbolt Pillager, featuring an Earth Crossbow Burst and rewarding **18 Archery XP**.
