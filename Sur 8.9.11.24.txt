**Patch/Changes 8.9.11.24** *(Network Changes)*
> :shiny: [Survival] Increased the buy prices of all **Cooked Food** to **$50** to prevent exploits involving the **Sellwand Multiplier**.
> :shiny: [Survival] Fixed **Liege Red** and **Liege Pink** not showing their respective prefixes in the **Tablist** for **Java Edition**.
> :shiny: [Survival] Updated the **Frostbound Trader** with **3 New Purchasable Slots**. Players can now purchase **Crate Keys**, **Runestones** & more!
> :shiny: [Survival] Improved the **Frostbound Trader** interface with a wider design and clearer information on how it works.
> :shiny: [Survival] **Frostbound** will now announce his restocks in chat and in the Discord alerts channel.
> :shiny: [Survival] Temporarily disabled the **Tag** showcase above players' heads due to a bug.
> :shiny: [Survival] Updated **Protection Stones** messages to look more modern, as they hadn't been changed since 2020.
> :shiny: [Survival] Modified **Mermaid, Peace, Beaconator, and more** lore.
> :shiny: [Survival] Added **Copper Chests and their variants** to **Protection Stone** protection.
> :shiny: [Survival] Added a new **Protection Stone Management Menu**, allowing players to manage their protections more easily.

> :shiny: [Survival] Added **Block Access Settings**, allowing players to control who can interact with or break specific blocks inside their protected areas.
> :shiny: [Survival] Players can now customize block permissions for **Visitors, Guests, and Co-Owners** with a simple click.
> :shiny: [Survival] Improved the **Members** and **Protection Flags** menus for easier navigation and management.
> :shiny: [Survival] Added **"/ps limits"** to check how many more Protection Stones you can place.
> :shiny: [Survival] Fixed the **Mass Vote Crate Opening** perk at **"/warp crates"**.
