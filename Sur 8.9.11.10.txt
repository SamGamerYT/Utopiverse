**Patch/Changes 8.9.11.10** *(Network Changes)*
> :shiny: [Survival] Increased the **[i] Cooldown** from **5 seconds** to **120 seconds**.
> :shiny: [Survival] Fixed an issue where players were unable to **fly properly** within their rented **Farm Plots**.
> :shiny: [Survival] Buffed the **Intrigue Defence** and **Intrigue Attack Runestones**.
> :shiny: [Survival] Reduced **Intrigue Set** requirements from **Defense 36** to **Defense 26**.
> :shiny: [Survival] Fixed an issue where players were not receiving the correct amount of **Tidecaller Upgrade Stones** from the **Tidecaller Lootbox**.
> :shiny: [Survival] Added **Submission Alerts** through **Discord**, **Titles**, and **Chat Messages** to ensure players are properly notified.
> :shiny: [Survival] Added a **Server Tip** explaining **How to Repair Crafted Gear** near **"/warp crates"**.
> :shiny: [Survival] Reduced **Draconic Looting V** crafting ingredient requirement of **4x Draconic Looting IV** to **2x Draconic Looting IV**.
> :shiny: [Survival] Reduced **Draconic Looting VI** crafting ingredient requirement of **4x Draconic Looting V** to **2x Draconic Looting V**.
> :shiny: [Survival] Fixed glitched **Chest & Player heads** in **26.2 Java Edition**.
