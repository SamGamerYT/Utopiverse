**Patch/Changes 8.9.11.06** *(Network Changes)*  
> :shiny: [Survival] Updated the style of **Server Tip** holograms so they now match one another.  
> :shiny: [Survival] Fixed an issue where **Soil Pots** were not accepting **Basic Speed Grow**.  
> :shiny: [Survival] Fixed an issue with mana abilities not executing properly.  
> :shiny: [Survival] Added the new **Annie** questline, located near the **Furniture Shop**.  
> :shiny: [Survival] Removed the **Confusion Key** from the **Exchanger Station**.  
> :shiny: [Survival] Removed **RPG Menu**, **FAQ**, **Tags Shop**, and **Quests Menu** from the **Bedrock Guide Menu**.  
> :shiny: [Survival] Renamed **Challenges** to **Milestones** in the **Bedrock Guide Menu**.  
> :shiny: [Survival] Fixed the **Settings Menu** not opening properly.  
> :shiny: [Survival] The **Dungeon Zone** option in the **Bedrock Guide Menu** now teleports players to **"/warp dungeons"**.  
> :shiny: [Survival] Renamed **The Tinkerer** to **Refine Shop** in **"/warps"**.  
> 
> :shiny: [Survival] Fixed **Refine Shop** and **AFK Zone** not unlocking within the **Regions Codex**.  
> :shiny: [Survival] Fixed a bug where players could water their **Soil Pots** using **Water Bottles**.  
> :shiny: [Survival] Fixed the **Close Icon** displaying incorrectly in the **"/sellg"** interface.  
> :shiny: [Survival] Added **Back on Death**, **Workbench**, and **Ender Chest** command upgrades to the **Upgrades NPC**.  
> :shiny: [Survival] **Singular Perks** and **Multi-Perks** upgrades are now separated.
