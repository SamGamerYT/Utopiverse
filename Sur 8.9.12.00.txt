**Patch/Changes 8.9.12.00** *(Network Changes)*
> :shiny: [Survival] Fixed an issue where **Auto Reel** Stars weren't being applied properly above Level 5.
> :shiny: [Survival] Added **Voice Chat** tutorial information near **"/spawn"**.
> :shiny: [Survival] Added a **Tempfly Reminder** notification.
> :shiny: [Survival] Added a **Protection Stone Filter** button to **"/ps locations"**.
> :shiny: [Survival] Fixed an exploit where players could skip **Shearing Sheep Scroll Quests** by shearing sheep inside someone else's Protection Stone.
> :shiny: [Survival] Added a **Spawn Map** poster near **"/warp bank"**.
> :shiny: [Survival] Updated the **Survival Announcements** with information for **Farms**, **Protection Stones**, **Daily Rewards**, **Challenges**, **Submissions** & more.
> :shiny: [Survival] Added **Spawn World**[https://map.utopiverse.net/spawn] & **Survival World**[https://map.utopiverse.net/survival] Online Map preview.
> :shiny: [Survival] Added the **"/psmap"** command, allowing players to show or hide specific Protection Stones and toggle their name visibility on the map!
> :shiny: [Survival] Players can now **Right Click** the **Repair Stones** to open an interface for repairing items.
