**Patch/Changes 8.9.11.08** *(Network Changes)*

> :shiny: [Survival] Reduced **Frostwyrm Axe** requirements from **Enchanting 11** to **0** and **Fighting 21** to **12**.
> :shiny: [Survival] Reduced **Scorched Ember Set** requirements from **Defense 30** to **21** and **Fighting 18** to **5**.
> :shiny: [Survival] Reduced **Arctic Owl Set** requirements from **Defense 30** to **20** and **Fighting 18** to **5**.
> :shiny: [Survival] Reduced **Frostwyrm Set** requirements from **Defense 30** to **14** and **Fighting 18** to **8**.
> :shiny: [Survival] **Scorched Ember Set** now includes the **CE COMPATIBLE** label.
> :shiny: [Survival] **Arctic Owl Set** now includes the **CE COMPATIBLE** label.
> :shiny: [Survival] **Frostwyrm Set** now includes the **CE COMPATIBLE** label.
> :shiny: [Survival] **Arctic Owl Set** now provides **Fire Defense** in addition to **Ice Defense**.
> :shiny: [Survival] **Frostwyrm Set** now provides **Earth Defense** in addition to **Ice Defense**.
> :shiny: [Survival] Reduced **Frostwyrm Set** rarity from **SPECIAL** to **MYTHICAL**.
>
> :shiny: [Survival] Updated **Player Tags** to display **Health** statistics.
> :shiny: [Survival] Fixed an issue where players were unable to claim **Deepslate Milestone #1** and **Cobblestone Milestone #1**.
> :shiny: [Survival] Fixed an issue with **Home Upgrade** where **Upgrade #2** would break the entire menu if the player lacked the required permission.
> :shiny: [Survival] Fixed an issue with **The Grand Antidote** questline where it would not accept **Fermented Baby Spooder Eyes**.
> :shiny: [Survival] Removed and disabled the **Duels System**.
> :shiny: [Survival] Removed the sell value from all duplicated **Blackmarket** furniture items.
> :shiny: [Survival] Reduced **Eagle Wings** requirements from **Defense 30** to **14** and **Agility 20** to **10**.
> :shiny: [Survival] Buffed **Volcano Invader Wings** stats from **5% Volcano Damage** to **6% Volcano Damage**, and **1% Volcano Boss Damage** to **2% Volcano Boss Damage**.
> :shiny: [Survival] Increased the **Volcano Invader Wings** tier from **Epic** to **Mythical**.
> :shiny: [Survival] Added the **Tidecaller Lootbox**, located at **"/warp dungeons"**.
>
> :shiny: [Survival] Patched a serious issue where players were able to stack **Global** and **Personal Boosters** together.
>
> **ENTITY CHANGES**
> :shiny: [Survival] Fixed a **fire rendering** issue affecting custom models on **Java Edition**.
