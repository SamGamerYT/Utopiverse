**Patch/Changes 2.0.21** *[Oneblock]*
> :grass: Server maintenance performed on **23-Aug-2026**.
> :grass: Fixed an issue with **Island Size Upgrades** where players with a higher level were unable to purchase the upgrade.
> :grass: Server maintenance performed on **26-Aug-2026**.
> :grass: Modified **"/welcome"**, **[!] Showcase Perk**, **Auto Assistance Responses**, and **Player Guide Gear**.
> :grass: Added **3 new automatic responses** covering **OneBlock Creation**, **OneBlock Border**, and **OneBlock Reset**.
> :grass: Added **Coinbound Ring**, **Opal Whisper Ring**, **Citrine Crusher Ring**, and **Ruby Ravager Ring** to the **Dreadrock Dungeon Crafting Station**.
> :grass: **Wither** now has a chance to drop **Hotfixer Repairstone** and **Compressed Rock**.
> :grass: **Warden** now has a chance to drop **Menders Repairstone** and **Compressed Rock**.
> :grass: Increased the durability gained when upgrading **Lost Miner Gear Set**, **Upgraded Miner Gear Set**, and **Refined Miner Gear Set** with **Metamorphic Rock** from **128** to **256**.
> :grass: **Lost Miner Gear Set** can now be upgraded up to **4 times**, increased from **2 times**.
> :grass: **Upgraded Miner Gear Set** can now be upgraded up to **6 times**, increased from **4 times**.
>
> :grass: **Refined Miner Gear Set** can now be upgraded up to **8 times**, increased from **6 times**.
> :grass: **Aegis Sapphire** will now increase **Defense** from **6** to **8**, while also granting an additional **4 Health**.
> :grass: Fixed **Mage Rank** missing the **"/sit"** command perk.
> :grass: Fixed **Rogue Rank** missing the **"/craft"** command perk.
