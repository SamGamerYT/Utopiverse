**Patch/Changes 2.0.23** *[Oneblock]*
> :grass: Fixed **"/warp tinkerer"**, **"/tinker"** & **"/refineshop"** not working properly.
> :grass: Fixed **Island Upgrade 250x250** not allowing players to upgrade their island at the required rank.
> :grass: Added **Voice Chat** tutorial information near **"/spawn"**.
> :grass: Interacting with the **Oneblock Start** block at **"/spawn"** will now teleport players to their **Oneblock**.
> :grass: Updated the **OneBlock Announcements** with the new **ANNOUNCER** format and refreshed information for **Ranks**, **Rewards**, **Teams**, **Dungeons**, **Bank**, **Blacksmith**, **Refine Shop** & **Tinkerer**.
> :grass: Reduced the **Blast Furnace** island value from **150** to **2**.
> :grass: **Hopper Speed** now matches that of **Survival**.
> :grass: Added **20 New Trivia** Questions, increasing the total to **160 Questions**.
> :grass: Updated **Monthly Crate** & **Lootboxes** messages.
> :grass: Fixed an issue where **Vote, Spawner, Lavender, Orchid & Cherry** crates were displaying random feathers.
> :grass: Updated the guaranteed reward system for **"/cherry"**.
